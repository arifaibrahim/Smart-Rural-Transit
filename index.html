About Smart Rural Transit & Prototype Scope

Routeless: The Last Mile empowers rural Dakshina Kannada students across 52 colleges/schools, 45 rural/urban stops, and 50 corridors to overcome overcrowding, skip-stop risks, breakdowns, and monsoon road closures.

Transparency & Verification Disclosure: Live GPS coordinates, Conductor ETM ticket streams, and depot spare-bus links are simulated in-browser for rapid hackathon validation. Official KSRTC schedule & hardware API verification is required before live district deployment.

# Smart Rural Transit — Routeless: The Last Mile

Dakshina Kannada rural student bus navigation and KSRTC emergency operations platform covering **52 educational institutions**, **45 stops**, and **50 routes**.

## Portals
- **Student Portal (`index.html`):** Passwordless travel type selection (**Normal Ticket** or **Student Bus Pass**), direct & 1-transfer backup routes, live GPS map, +15 min exam precautionary buffer, and waiting beacon.
- **KSRTC Official Portal (`ksrtc.html`):** Separate authenticated operations portal for Bus Breakdown reporting, Nearest Depot Spare Bus Dispatch, Traffic Police Road Closure & Detours, and Traffic Congestion / +40% Demand Surge controls.
